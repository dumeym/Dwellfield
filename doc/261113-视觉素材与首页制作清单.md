## 用户已校正的外观关系

- 长立面**左侧为玻璃门**，其门口有**浅木色 2 m × 4 m timber deck**。
- 长立面**右侧为窗**，不是第二扇落地门；中间保留窄窗。屋面为深色双坡屋顶，墙面为暖木色木纹铝板，主体为轻钢结构。
- Deck 在当前商业配置中属于**付费选配**。因此带 deck 的主图上站时须明确写 *Shown with optional timber deck*；不能让客户误认为 AUD150,000 标配已含 deck。
- 已删除第一版“右侧误画成门”的工作区主图，避免误用。原始生成记录仍留在 Codex 的生成图片目录，不用于网站。

## 图片生成提示词记录

### 首页概念图：原始生成

> Use case: sketch-to-render. Premium Australian granny flat website hero photograph. Architectural sketch governs the building form and openings; prior warm-cladding concept is mood and colour reference. Single-storey 12 m by 4.5 m secondary dwelling in a leafy Queensland backyard, simple dark gabled roof, slim black frames, warm honey-oak timber-look aluminium cladding, realistic scale and construction, high-end editorial natural light, wide landscape, no people, text, logos or watermark.

### 首页概念图：用户指正后的最终编辑

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Preserve the camera angle, long gabled dark roof, warm timber-look aluminium cladding, backyard and photographic quality. On the long front elevation, keep the LEFT large black-framed glazed double door as entrance. Directly outside it add one low PALE NATURAL WOOD deck, about 4 m along the facade by 2 m projecting into the lawn. Change the RIGHT glazed double door to a BLACK-FRAMED WINDOW of similar width with a solid wall section below it. Keep the middle narrow window and left gable end square window. No extra doors, decks, awning, people or text.

### 室内精修

> Use case: lighting-weather. Edit the real completed-interior photo as careful architectural photo retouch: neutralise colour cast, brighten shadows, recover highlights, subtly straighten perspective and retain a natural mood. Preserve the actual layout, white kitchen, sink, cooktop, cabinetry, dark trim, ceiling light, dining table, hallway, floor, glazed door, TV and laundry appliances. Do not invent additional windows, taller ceilings, larger room, new furniture or premium fixtures. No text or watermark.

### 起居室精修

> Use case: lighting-weather. Architectural editorial photo retouch of the real prototype living area. Preserve the same geometry and camera angle, sofa below the black-framed window, dining table and chairs, oval coffee table, wall TV, air conditioner, ceiling light, wall panels and floor. Improve balanced daylight, neutral warm whites, natural contrast and perspective. Do not enlarge or redesign the room, add architectural features, replace fixtures or move furniture. No text or watermark.
